# Privacy and data safety

OfferLoop Assist sends authenticated requests to the OfferLoop service for transcription and answer generation. Account balance, device connections, payment references, usage records, and service audit records are managed by the OfferLoop server.

The desktop stores its OfferLoop session using operating-system protected storage. Customers are not asked to store provider API keys in the application.

Before using audio or screenshot features:

- Obtain any consent required by participants and local law.
- Follow interview, employer, school, and meeting policies.
- Do not capture credentials, payment information, health information, private messages, customer records, or confidential company material.
- Review generated answers for accuracy.

The controlling policy is the current OfferLoop privacy policy at https://www.offerloop.in/privacy-policy.
